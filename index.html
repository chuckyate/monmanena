Setup

Three steps, and it's part of your desktop for good.

01
Pick a gadget from the tray
Open the tray with a corner swipe or a shortcut — every gadget you own lives there.
02
Drag it onto your desktop
It snaps to a grid so tiles line up, but you can free-place if you'd rather.
03
Resize, recolor, and forget it
Layouts save themselves. Reboot, and every tile is exactly where you left it.
Appearance

Every tile takes your color.

Set an accent once and it runs through the whole tray — clock hands, meter fills, note edges, all of it.
